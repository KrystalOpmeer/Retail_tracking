How the QR Platform Works

A simple three-step strategy to capture consumer intent, manage product promotions, and track conversions.

1. Define Asset & Store

Register display stands, shelves, kiosks, or endcaps. Specify the physical store and upload banners for the stand.

2. Attach Product Listings

Link items currently on display. Manage detailed specifications, active pricing, images, and special checkout offers.

3. Auto-Generate & Scan

A unique QR code is created immediately. Place it on the stand; customers scan to view products and track scanner analytics.
